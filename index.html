Task4Money — Child Safety Standards

Policy and standards against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)
Last updated: 5 June 2026

Task4Money is committed to providing a safe environment for all users. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page describes the standards we uphold and how concerns can be reported, in compliance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

1. Our commitment

Task4Money strictly prohibits any content, behavior, or activity that sexualizes, endangers, or exploits minors. This includes, without limitation:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form;
  • Grooming, solicitation, sextortion, or any attempt to engage a minor in sexual activity;
  • Sexualized depictions or descriptions of minors;
  • Trafficking, endangerment, or exploitation of children.

2. Age eligibility

Task4Money is intended for adult users only. Accounts found to belong to individuals below the minimum age permitted by our Terms of Service are removed.

3. Prevention and moderation

  • Users can report inappropriate content or behavior directly within the app.
  • Reported content is reviewed and, where warranted, removed; the responsible account is suspended or permanently banned.
  • We act on credible reports of CSAE without delay.

4. Reporting a concern

Anyone can report a child safety concern through the in-app support / report function, or by contacting our designated point of contact below. Reports are treated confidentially and reviewed promptly.

5. Response and cooperation with authorities

When we identify or are made aware of apparent CSAM or CSAE, we remove the content, take action against the responsible account, and cooperate with applicable law enforcement and relevant child-protection authorities (including, where appropriate, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, NCMEC) in accordance with applicable law.
